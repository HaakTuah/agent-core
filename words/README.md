# The owner's words behind the core

The owner's own words behind items 1 to 245 of the core's archive, each under its item's number, with the game and
archive entry it came from: an Amarna Letters entry by its item number, a Barkstreet Snakes entry by its date. They
stay in the core's own repository alone: `.gitattributes` keeps this folder out of every project's copy, which carries
each item's reasoning in the core's words instead (`archive/instructions.md`; `AGENTS.md`, "The archive keeps the
reasoning; the owner's words stay here").

The text between the owner's quotes is a short note of what the owner was answering, in plain words; only what is
inside quotation marks is the owner's. A bare yes is not quoted: the item says the owner agreed, and to what. Words
addressed to a session (praise and titles, such as "expert") are left out of a quotation, with no mark where they
stood. "[…]" marks a personal detail left out (`floor/security.md`, rule 11); "(…)" marks a part about one game only,
left in that game's archive; "[...]" is a gap the game's own archive left in the quote.

Nothing is added here: from item 246 on, the owner's words stay on the issue the item names. Item N is in the file
whose first number is the highest at or below N.
