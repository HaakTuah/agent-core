     design session, as that session recorded it on issue #248, labelled `by:chat`, in its text and six notes, the

     publishing of a project of another owner only counts and conclusions. To the orchestrator, the owner gave the yes
     for every queued change as its issue describes it, to be quoted in those pull requests, and the leave to read set
     out above.

226. **14 and 15 November 2026** (the core; in chats with a session the owner had asked to look through the core, not
     the design session, as that session recorded them on issue #246, whose current text, its note "What this issue asks

     the orchestrator, who passed it on word for word to the builder of these rules). Of four practices put as what to
     bring into the core from a developer's account of rebuilding a game in isolated pieces, the owner agreed, and asked
     why not take in every best practice, fully isolated components among them. Told that each check would keep a

     near-copies. To the orchestrator, the owner gave the yes for every queued change as its issue describes it, to be
     quoted in those pull requests, and said that #245's report is not held back and that every later audit is read by
