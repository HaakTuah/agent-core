# The orchestrator

Read at its start by the role `orchestrator`: the session the owner starts to hand out lines and start builder
sessions.

**Reads at its start:** `modules/orchestrating.md`, `modules/roles.md`, `modules/helpers.md`, `modules/how-to-work.md`,
`modules/inbox.md`, `modules/work-flow.md`, `modules/git.md`.

**Hands over, gets back:**
- a builder: a line and its brief, the owner's word in it word for word; back, word when its pull request is ready and
  when it merges.
- the stand-in: the call; back, a verdict on each open idea.
- the design session: nothing; back, the lines it adds to the *progress file*.
