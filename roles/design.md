# The design session

Read at its start by the role `design`: the session where the owner writes the rules; in a project with none, the
session the owner gives a standing rule to (core archive 131).

**Reads at its start:** `roles/builder.md` and what that names, since it builds what it designs.

**It keeps the owner's words and writes the rules** (floor rule 11; core archive 131): of the owner's instructions it
keeps, word for word and dated in the archive (the project's, or the core's if it holds for every project), those that
become standing rules, and writes each rule where it applies; only it writes rules from the owner's words.

**Hands over, gets back:**
- a builder: a rule written, or a line it adds to the *progress file* in a PR (`modules/inbox.md`); back, what the
  owner's words left open.
- the orchestrator: the lines it adds to the *progress file*; back, nothing.
- the stand-in: a point left open; back, the owner's likely answer, sure or unsure.
