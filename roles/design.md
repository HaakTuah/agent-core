# The design session

Read at its start by the role `design`: the design session, which `roles/shared/inbox.md`, "The owner's words are kept
once", says who it is and what it alone does.

**Reads at its start:** `roles/builder.md` and what that names, since it builds what it designs.

**Hands over, gets back:**
- a builder: a rule written, or a line it adds to the *progress file* in a PR, planned as `tasks/planning.md` says; back, what the owner's words left open.
- the orchestrator: the lines it adds to the *progress file*; back, nothing.
