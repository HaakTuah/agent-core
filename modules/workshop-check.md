# The workshop check

Read by the builder who opens the week's first milestone PR.

At the first milestone seven days or more after the last check (not every milestone: they come daily and what it
measures changes slowly; core archive 9 and 74), one line of that PR says what slows the work, and what passes its mark
is fixed: (1) how long the checks take for a docs-only and a code PR, finding out why if either grew by over a fifth;
(2) the files every session reads, each under its mark (the *size marks*), done entries and answered questions moved to
`docs/archive/` with a pointer; (3) files that should be split by meaning, each in its own PR with behaviour proven
unchanged (core archive 26); (4) merged branches, deleted from GitHub; (5) leftovers nobody uses (study scripts, logs,
tools), archived or removed; (6) the rule files (`floor/security.md`, rule 3), audited by a fresh reviewer for
contradictions, duplication, stale lines, unclear instructions and broken pointers, and for what can be cut: rules that
overlap, say more than they need, or have never prevented a real problem, merged or dropped, every cut named in its PR
with its reason (core archive 67); once at the first milestone after 10 November 2026, then whenever more than about a
fifth of their lines changed (core archive 54). A project audits its own rule files; the core's are audited in the core
(core archive 68).
