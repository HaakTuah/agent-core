- **27 November 2026, one orchestrator works a queue by a lease.** Nothing to do by hand (#1106). From this sync on, a project's orchestrator holds a lease on one issue labelled `orchestrator` (`core/tasks/tools.md`, "One orchestrator works a queue, by a lease, with its hand-off beside it"): at every wake, before anything else, it runs `node core/scripts/lease.mjs --session <its own> --tool <its tool> --builders <how many run>`, which, at its first wake after the sync, opens that issue, the hand-off's frame its text, and takes the lease; where the script prints "Stop:", another session holds the queue, and it deletes its wakes and stops. The issue's current text is its hand-off, kept current at each change. Its wake comes within the cache's hour, 55 minutes, while a builder runs or a review is due, set again at each wake, in place of the hourly wake set once to repeat; with nothing in flight it keeps none. Whoever starts an orchestrator (the owner, the adviser) runs `node core/scripts/lease.mjs` first, and starts one only where it says one may be. The label is made with the rest of the core's list by the look's job (`core/scripts/labels.mjs --make`), and the scheduled look reports a lease renewed by two sessions and wakes more than an hour apart while builders ran. A project with no orchestrator has nothing to do. The cost: a comment on the lease issue at each wake, and one read of the host.
