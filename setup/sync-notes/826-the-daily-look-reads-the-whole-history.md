From one commit those counted from that commit alone, and once a reader refuses a shallow clone (#1422), the cleaning task stood unread at every run. A project whose `.github/workflows/look.yml` is the kit's has nothing to do: the sync's own process moves its call to the copy's commit. One that keeps its call off the sync moves its pin in one small pull request, a workflow's change with floor rule 3's two security reviews. A project whose orchestrator runs the look in place of the daily job has no step to take: where its clone is shallow, the look's readers say so, with the mend `git fetch --unshallow`. The cost: a checkout of the whole history in the daily job, a few seconds for most projects, no job added.
