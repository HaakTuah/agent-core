- **26 November 2026, the daily look reads the whole history.** Nothing to do (#1426). From this commit on, the core's `look.yml` checks out the project's whole history (`fetch-depth: 0`) where it took one commit, since the look's readers count it: the dose's cleaning share and how often each file changed (`core/scripts/dose.mjs`), and the day the labels' check came (`core/scripts/labels.mjs`). From one commit those counted from that commit alone, and once a reader refuses a shallow clone (#1422), the cleaning task stood unread at every run. The sync that brings it moves each call of the core's `look.yml` to the copy's commit, since the workflow changed, in the kit's `look.yml` and in any other workflow of the project's that calls it, and names each it cannot read. A project whose orchestrator runs the look in place of the daily job has no step to take: where its clone is shallow, the look's readers say so, with the mend `git fetch --unshallow`. The cost: a checkout of the whole history in the daily job, a few seconds for most projects, no job added.
