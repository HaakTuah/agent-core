# The reasons behind the core: issue #1413.2

The index, and how an item is written: `archive/instructions.md`.

#1413.2. **26 November 2026**, #1413, point 2: a design the adviser set out to the owner, who agreed to it with its
     limits. Where the owner agrees to a job (a batch, a release, a trial, a set of lines), that yes covers every step
     of it as set out: no session asks again for a step the rules or that yes already settle, and the orchestrator
     decides such a step itself and reports it where it works rather than waiting on the adviser. It never stretches to
     a yes floor rule 14 names (a step that cannot be undone, money beyond what the owner set aside, a change to the
     floor or the review guidelines beyond what was agreed), nor past the app's own permission prompts, which no rule
     removes. The reasoning: a question whose answer a standing word already gives is a pure formality, and the owner
     wants none; what is covered is done and reported. It stands behind "One yes covers a whole job" in
     `roles/orchestrator.md`, which takes in the rule "It asks the owner nothing" that stood there, and behind "It asks;
     it does not assume" in `roles/adviser.md`.
